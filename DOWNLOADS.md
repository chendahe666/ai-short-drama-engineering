## 校验与打印 / Verify and print

Windows: `Get-FileHash <downloaded-file> -Algorithm SHA256`

macOS/Linux: `shasum -a 256 <downloaded-file>`

与SHA256SUMS.txt比较。可在书站打印当前章节，使用浏览器“另存为PDF”。本版没有独立排版PDF或EPUB。

Compare the result with SHA256SUMS.txt. Print any chapter from the book site and choose your browser's Save as PDF option. This release has no separately typeset PDF or EPUB.
