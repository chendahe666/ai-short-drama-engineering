## Method and case

[Knowledge map](appendices/en/overview.md) · [Back for Revision](appendices/en/case.md) · [20 draft standards](appendices/en/standards.md) · [70 sources and access limits](research/README.md) · [Project production skill](skills/ai-short-drama-production/SKILL.md)

The method connects a problem, evidence, applicable conditions, an action, acceptance and revision. External obligations, project agreements, conditional recommendations and untested hypotheses remain distinct. Platform revenue, creator receipts, project contribution and net profit use separate definitions. Model self-ratings do not establish audience comprehension or commercial results.

## Bilingual maintenance

Chinese source manuscripts live in `manuscript/zh`; English translations live in `manuscript/en`. A shared generator maintains 72 chapter pages in `book`. Stable identifiers such as `B02-C03` pair chapters. Source hashes flag translations for review when their source changes.

[Translation status](TRANSLATION-STATUS.md) · [Editing and build guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) · [Publishing references](PUBLISHING-REFERENCES.md)

The English edition is AI-assisted, with structural and citation-completeness checks plus selective cross-review. It has not undergone professional human translation review. Original research notes and structured evidence retain their source language; the research index identifies their scope rather than claiming all notes are translated.
