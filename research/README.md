本目录保留2026-10-13首轮检索的来源与阅读边界。70个唯一来源ID分为CM（国内）、GM（海外）、TS（工具与技能）、FM（方法基础）。研究笔记主要为中文；原始URL指向各语言原文。未取得的全文、不可读评论、宣传性质和抽样限制均保留在记录中。

This directory preserves the first research round and its access limits. Seventy unique source IDs use CM (China), GM (international), TS (tools/skills), and FM (methodological foundations). Notes are primarily Chinese; URLs link to original materials. Missing full texts, unread comments, promotional provenance and sampling limits remain explicit. These notes are not a second English textbook and have not all been translated.
