# Conditional production principles

These are working principles synthesized for this project. They do not claim universal industry consensus.

| Decision | Default | When to reconsider | Observable check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story progression | Make changes in knowledge, choices or relationships legible | Deliberate repetition, atmosphere, absurdism | Can viewers explain what changed? |
| Evidence in plot | Establish access and reveal before dependent action | Dramatic irony, later explained unreliable narration | Character/audience knowledge ledger |
| Episode ending | Resolve some promise and create an intentional endpoint | Standalone stories may fully close | What was answered, what remains, why continue? |
| Spatial continuity | Keep gaze/position readable across cuts | Intended disorientation or viewpoint change | View sequence, ask who faces whom |
| Shot length | Match action, speech, reading and emotion | Tool limits or stylistic rhythm | Actual performance and edit, not numeric rule alone |
| Generation complexity | Probe uncertain interactions before volume | Existing accepted benchmark is relevant | Failure types and accepted output, by model/version |
| Text in picture | Prefer editable accurate layers for crucial evidence | Stylized unreadability is intended | Dates/names and small-screen legibility |
| Sound | Design voice, space and meaningful non-speech sounds | Intentional silence or nonrealist treatment | Listen alone, then with picture |
| Feedback | Treat comments as hypotheses with context | Representative verified data may support stronger inference | Source, selection, counterexamples and test |
| Profitability | Use actual contract and all relevant costs | Explicit hypothetical scenario only | Reconcile receipts, rights and spending |
| Rule updates | Revise the narrow failing rule and its dependencies | New evidence warrants a broader change | Before/after record and regression case |

Evidence anchors: [Adobe continuity](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/video/discover/what-is-the-180-degree-rule.html), [YouTube retention](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en), [W3C captions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/captions-prerecorded), [Microsoft experimentation](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/patterns-of-trustworthy-experimentation-pre-experiment-stage/), [Audience in the Loop — interview limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14045v1).
