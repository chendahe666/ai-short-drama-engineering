# Chapter 2: What to Check in Existing Knowledge

Building a disciplinary framework requires acknowledging prior work. Film, animation, screenwriting, editing, sound, production management, and user research already have their own methods. Short drama presents a particular combination of brief duration, mobile viewing, platform distribution, and generative production. Research should identify which conditions have changed; renaming an existing method does not establish an invention.

This search found a Bilibili AI short-drama course listing covering scripts, storyboards, image-to-video generation, voice work, and editing. However, the page could not be read, so the quality of the video teaching and its comments was not verified. It is therefore a lead showing that a course exists, not validated teaching evidence. It certainly must not become a claim that “the author has studied the entire course.” [Course-listing lead](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1KCgF6gEKs/)

The public PDF of T/CEAP 1—2025, *Technical Specification for Microdrama Creative Production and Content Review*, covers pre-production, production, post-production, review, and archiving. Its cover states that the China Ethnic Film and Television Art Development Promotion Association issued it in October 2025. However, the National Library of Standards search page marks the same number as “withdrawn,” and the classification scheme in the old text cannot substitute for the 2026 regulations. This establishes a precedent for standardization while limiting the document to a historical reference. The replacement text and withdrawal date have not been confirmed. [Historical text](https://www.tvoao.com/userfiles/files/%E3%80%8A%E5%BE%AE%E7%9F%AD%E5%89%A7%E5%88%9B%E4%BD%9C%E7%94%9F%E4%BA%A7%E5%8F%8A%E5%86%85%E5%AE%B9%E5%AE%A1%E6%A0%B8%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF%E8%A7%84%E8%8C%83%E3%80%8B.pdf) [Standards-library status](https://www.ndls.org.cn/standard/index?draftman=%E9%9F%A6%E7%8A%81)

This is also a complete research iteration: discover a text, verify its date and issuer, continue checking its current status, find a conflict, and narrow the conclusion. Our contribution should be described as “a pilot, inspectable production framework for AI short drama.” Deliver material that others can use first, then discuss whether it can gain wider acceptance. Saving, publishing, or downloading a draft does not establish industry consensus.

**Exercise.** Does finding an old standard and ten articles repeating it justify calling it currently valid?  
**Suggested answer.** No. Repeated articles may all derive from one source. Check the original issuer, status, replacement version, and scope. When status is unclear, cite the document for information only.
