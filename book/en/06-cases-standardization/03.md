# Chapter 3: Bringing Real Brand Cases into a Textbook

A case card must distinguish “what was observed” from “what someone says happened.” Kuaishou's 2026 interim results disclosed that Kling's second-quarter revenue exceeded CNY 850 million, while short-drama supply and related advertising spending also grew. This indicates commercial connections among tools, platforms, and content supply, but does not disclose the net profit of an ordinary production team's work. It cannot turn “selling tools generates revenue” into “making any short drama is profitable.” [Kuaishou results](https://ir.kuaishou.com/zh-hans/news-releases/news-release-details/kuaishoukejifabu2026niandierjidujizhongqiweijingshenhecaiwuyeji)

In an attributed interview, Huang Chujie described early projects requiring multiple people, several months, and extensive character refinement. In another written interview, Jiuzhou Culture described its production at scale and identified consistency, fine control, and cultural adaptation as problems. Both accounts are useful for designing workflows, but differences in costs may reflect duration, period, image quality, division of labor, and what the accounting includes. Align the specifications before comparing efficiency. Without invoices and a complete cost definition, retain the label “interviewee self-report.” [Huang Chujie interview](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33101093) [Jiuzhou written interview](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33955220)

Platform rules also require reading their conditions. iQIYI's new “multi-episode short film” route requires no more than three episodes and a total duration of at least sixty minutes. Three one-minute pilot episodes do not qualify just because the episode count matches. Douyin's Short Drama Creator Center provides entry points for IP, submissions, matching, and collaboration; its homepage describes earnings as subject to agreement. An entry point's existence does not mean that any selected novel is already licensed. [iQIYI announcement](https://pages.iqiyi.com/p/scpr/63x0c36f.html) [Short Drama Creator Center](https://www.shortdramas.com/)

An effective case card records the project and period, audience and channel, delivery specifications, rights arrangements, work steps, observed results, missing data, and mechanisms that may transfer elsewhere. At present, *Back for Revision* can only be a design case about establishing approval evidence. It cannot enter a collection of “validated hits.”

**Exercise.** One team says it finished an entire drama in a week, while another took three months. Which is more efficient?  
**Suggested answer.** There is not enough information. First check finished duration, quality criteria, staff hours, prefabricated assets, failed attempts, and whether writing and review are included. Then calculate the total cost per usable finished minute.
