# Volume 6: Case Studies and Standardization Practice

*From a Production Experience to Shared, Revisable Rules*

Pilot edition 0.1 | Research cutoff: 2026-10-13

This volume teaches you how to study real cases, test production principles, and write standards proposals. It works alongside the other volumes on markets, narrative, audiovisual craft, generation, and distribution. Its focus is making an experience something that others can inspect, reproduce, and challenge. After completing the six chapters, you should be able to submit a rule with evidence, a defined scope, and a checking method—not merely a suggestion that “this works better.”

The recurring case, *Back for Revision*, is an original teaching story created for this project. Designer Lu Qiao is accused of putting the wrong opening date on promotional materials, and supervisor Han Zheng pressures her to sign a responsibility form. She uses the v7 and v8 approval records to check the facts with client Zhou Ning and discovers that the supervisor skipped confirmation of the client's changed date. The problem is eventually resolved by correcting the process and reprinting the materials, but saving the project still has a cost. This volume discusses how the case should be reviewed; the detailed script and shots are in the case files. The teaching case has not been filmed and has no audience or revenue data. It is not an adaptation of the novel recommended earlier. AI adaptation rights for that earlier novel remain unverified.
